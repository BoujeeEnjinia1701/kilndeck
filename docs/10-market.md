# KilnDeck market and viability

KilnDeck is for the fixed-chimney and zigzag brick kilns of South Asia, where firemen still feed fuel on foot on the kiln top; nothing on the market gives them a safe place to stand, so the benchmark is the general work platforms a kiln owner could adapt and the motorised feeders that research programmes have tried. An open design matters because the users are poor, the kilns are many and scattered, and the fabricators who would build it are local workshops, not equipment makers. Prices and benchmarks are cited with the date they were checked. Method: STANDARDS section 23.

Cost notes: steel is costed at USD 1.30 per kilogram in the BOM, an estimate; an Indian listing for mild steel rectangular pipe was INR 53 per kilogram (IndiaMART, Anjani Tubes, Ghaziabad, checked 2026-10-25). A 100 mm, 20 mm round-groove gate wheel rated 400 kg sells at GBP 12.20 before VAT in the UK (F.H. Brundle, checked 2026-10-25); the BOM's USD 30 for a 500 kg wheel is a regional estimate to confirm (KND-DEC-001 item 3). Fabrication labour is not included.

## Open route

Who builds it: a steel fabricator in a brick district, from the drawings, the build plan and the BOM, paid by the kiln owner or a programme. How it spreads: through clean-kiln programmes and kiln owners' associations that already run training, and through the open repository (CERN-OHL-S-2.0 hardware, MIT software), so any workshop can build, change and share it. What keeps it open: the strongly reciprocal licence, which requires changes to be shared, and a design that uses only stock steel and catalogue bearings, wheels and chain. KilnDeck is a non-commercial open engineering reference; no sale is planned.
