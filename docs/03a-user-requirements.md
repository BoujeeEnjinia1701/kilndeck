# KilnDeck user requirements

What firemen, kiln owners and fabricators need, written from their side. The product requirements that answer them are in KND-PRS-001 (`03-requirements.md`), and the links are in the generated traceability matrix KND-RTM-001 (`03b-traceability.md`). Method: STANDARDS section 22. These needs are a desk reading of the problem statement; the first co-design task (interviews with firemen) will confirm or change them.

*Table 1. User requirements.*

| ID | Category | Title | Need | Priority | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UR-001 | Safety | Off the crust | I feed every hole without standing or walking on the ash crust over the fire | High | Kiln fireman (PRB Table 1a) |
| UR-002 | Safety | No fall | I cannot fall from where I work, even if I slip or lean on the rail | High | Kiln fireman |
| UR-003 | Performance | Keep the round | Feeding a full round takes no longer than it does now, and the fuel goes into the hole, not onto the crust | High | Kiln fireman; kiln owner |
| UR-004 | Usability | By hand | I move and feed with my own effort; there is no power on the kiln top | High | Kiln owner (PRB constraints) |
| UR-005 | Safety | Less heat | Less heat reaches my feet and body than standing on the crust | High | Kiln fireman |
| UR-006 | Performance | Steady dose | Each hole gets about the same amount of coal or biomass | Medium | Clean-kiln programme; kiln owner |
| UR-007 | Deployment and assembly | Fits my kiln | It fits the kiln I have and loads only the sound walls, never the crust | High | Kiln owner |
| UR-008 | Deployment and assembly | Two-person carry | Two people carry every piece up the kiln and bolt it together there | High | Local fabricator; kiln owner |
| UR-009 | Logistics | Moves with the fire | It follows the fire round the kiln as it moves each day | Medium | Kiln owner |
| UR-010 | Cost | Affordable | It costs little next to a season's firing margin | Medium | Kiln owner |
| UR-011 | Deployment and assembly | Local build | A local workshop builds it from stock steel with hand tools, a drill and a stick welder | Medium | Local fabricator |
| UR-012 | Environment and durability | Season-proof | It survives heat, coal dust, ash, rain and the storms of a firing season | Medium | Kiln owner |
| UR-013 | Safety | Stays put | It cannot run away, leave its track or blow along the kiln while I am on it or while it is parked | High | Kiln fireman; kiln owner |
| UR-014 | Maintenance | Easy upkeep | Jams can be cleared and parts greased or replaced by the firemen or a local fitter | Low | Kiln owner; local fabricator |

Categories: deployment and assembly, performance, usability, safety, environment and durability, logistics, maintenance, cost, regulatory. Priorities: High, Medium, Low.
