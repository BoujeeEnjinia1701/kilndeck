# 0004: Decisions of 2026-10-30

- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Status:** accepted (D10, D11, D14); D16 proposed, awaiting Amish

## Context

## What the recommendation did not foresee

The recommendation said every piece would be under 33 kg. The detailed model gives: track modules 26.7 kg, middle truss modules 32.9 kg, floor panels 24.8 kg, outer end truck 29.8 kg, but the end truss modules 34.4 kg (side A) and 34.8 kg (side B), because each carries a 10 mm foot plate of 1.9 kg. PR-012 is therefore recorded as not met by 1.4 to 1.8 kg, and STEM rows S17 and S22 as fails with a fix proposed. This does not change the working principle, pitch or scope. Options are proposed in KND-DEC-001 D16: bolt the foot plates on (recommended, no margin), six modules a side, or accept the shortfall.

## Not decided

- D16, above: new, no decision.
- The approach to a co-design partner (KND-DDR-001, D8) is an action for Amish, not a proposal with a recommendation.

## Consequences

- Option A's model changed, so the options picture (`media/options.png`), the product renders and the cards need re-rendering by the coordinator; they are not made here.
- The wind rule is a rule for the operator; the independent review found the hand force used up at about 5 m/s on a 2 % grade, so a graded kiln needs a lower limit, which the partner survey sets.
